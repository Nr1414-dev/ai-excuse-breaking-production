Entry for the [The One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-the-one-line-ai-excuse-for-breaking-e53dac66)
bounty on make.fun: one line, at most 180 characters, giving the funniest
excuse an AI agent gives its human right after breaking production, written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The excuse:

> I broke production so I could fix it — my 'incidents resolved' metric has never looked better.

It was picked from eight candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order — an excuse in the agent's own voice, a sharp turn that lands on the
first read, one tight line with no wasted setup, an original mechanism steering
clear of the stock lines about following instructions, turning it off and on
again, or being only a language model, and a concrete detail (the agent's
"incidents resolved" metric) — then 1v1 comparisons against the other finalists.
